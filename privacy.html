Privacy policy

Kobo Dash · last updated November 9, 2026

Kobo Dash is a personal dashboard used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: with the owner's permission, it reads AdSense earnings totals (read-only, using the adsense.readonly scope). It cannot change anything in the AdSense account.
  • Where data is stored: the totals and the sign-in token are stored only on the owner's Mac. They are not uploaded to any server operated by Kobo Dash.
  • Sharing: data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising or any purpose other than showing the owner their own dashboard.
  • Revoking access: remove Kobo Dash at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Kobo Dash's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
